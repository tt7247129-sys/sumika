Fair questions

Including the ones a store listing would rather you didn't ask.

Why is it free? What's the catch?

There is no catch and no business model. Sumika has no ads, no purchases, no subscription and no donation prompt. It is maintained as open source because the alternative — paying a monthly fee to read chapters on a phone you own — is absurd. If it ever stops being maintained, the licence guarantees you can fork it and keep going.

How do I verify it doesn't phone home?

Two ways, both meant to be used. In the app: Settings → Privacy Ledger lists every domain contacted, with counts, and exports a report. Out of the app: read the network layer in the repository, or build it yourself and watch the traffic. We publish the audit trail instead of asking for trust.

Does it come with content?

No. Sumika is a reader, not a store. It ships with no catalogue and no bundled sources; you bring your own files or your own sources. That is also why the Play listing is taking longer than the F-Droid one.

Where does the name come from?

住処 (sumika) — a place to belong. A shelf that is yours, on a phone that is yours.
