Sumika is a free and open-source manga reader built for people who want full control over their library. No ads. No tracking. No subscriptions.

<b>YOUR LIBRARY, YOUR RULES</b>
• Browse hundreds of community sources through the extension system, or read your own CBZ/ZIP/CBR files
• Organize with categories, filters and smart sorting
• Automatic library updates keep your favorite series fresh
• Portable backups (.tachibk compatible) — migrate from Tachiyomi, Mihon and friends in one tap

<b>A READER BUILT FOR COMICS</b>
• RTL, LTR, continuous vertical (webtoon) and paged reading modes
• Per-series overrides: reading mode, page fit, background color and more
• Download chapters for fully offline reading
• Material 3 interface optimized for phones, tablets and foldables

<b>PRIVACY FIRST</b>
• No advertising or analytics SDKs — telemetry is disabled at the build level and cannot be re-enabled
• Privacy Ledger: an on-device log of the app's network activity, fully inspectable at any time
• Optional parental mode shows only safe content and locks the settings behind authentication
• Back up to any folder or cloud storage mounted on your device — no account required

<b>FOLLOW YOUR SERIES</b>
• Tracking integration with AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu and Shikimori
• Per-library update schedules with notifications

Sumika is localized in 70+ languages and licensed under GPL-3.0, developed by volunteers. Sumika itself hosts no content: you choose which sources to install, and you are responsible for using them in accordance with the law and publishers' terms in your region.
